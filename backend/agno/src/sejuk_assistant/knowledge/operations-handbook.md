# Roles and responsibilities

## Manager

Managers oversee service quality and operational performance. They:

- Review completed service jobs.
- Accept completed work or return it for correction.
- Explain which work or checklist items technicians must redo.
- Monitor postponements and operational issues requiring action.
- Review technician performance.
- Close reviewed service orders.
- Decide operational exceptions and escalations.

## Admin

Admins coordinate service orders and payment administration. They:

- Create service orders and maintain customer details.
- Assign technicians.
- Monitor customer payment accounts.
- Record subsequent customer payments.
- Add payment remarks and follow up on outstanding balances.

## Technician

Technicians perform assigned field-service work. They:

- Start and complete assigned jobs.
- Complete required checklist items.
- Add work notes and evidence.
- Record payments received during a field visit.
- Postpone a visit with a reason when necessary.
- Correct work returned by a manager.

# Customer payment issues

## Partial payment or outstanding balance

Contact an admin first. The admin records the payment arrangement, adds remarks,
and follows up on the outstanding balance.

## Dispute, refund, discount, waiver, or incorrect final amount

Escalate the issue to a manager. A technician or admin must not promise a refund,
discount, or balance waiver without manager approval.

## Payment received during field service

The assigned technician may record the amount and payment method received from
the customer.

## Payment follow-up notes

Record the amount received, remaining balance, payment method, agreed follow-up
date, and relevant customer remarks.
